Two passes: the card base and layers, then the droplet scissored to its own bounding box so the raymarch only runs where the droplet actually is.

Refraction sets optical thickness in pixels and samples the shader-generated background, not the DOM. Chromatic dispersion scales with it, since the colour split is a fraction of the bend. At zero the droplet keeps its sheen and rim but stops bending anything.

Save writes the current settings to localStorage and they load on refresh. Copy config emits the same values as component props for porting into the real callout. Reset clears storage and returns to the values this page shipped with.

The text ghost is the callout's own copy rasterized to a 2D canvas — computed font, weight, colour and layout box read straight off each element, re-wrapped at the real width. It composites inside card(), so the droplet refracts it along with everything else. The real DOM text sits above the canvas, untouched and selectable.

Sheen speed drives its own clock, independent of the pattern motion toggle, so the surface can roll while the shaders underneath stay still. Light angle rotates the specular around the droplet; 0° puts it at the top.

Five ambient blobs per callout drift on slow Lissajous paths, seeded per variant so no two callouts breathe in sync. They melt into the slab and the cursor droplet through the same smin.

Ambient life scales drift speed, travel distance, and how far blobs rise out of the slab. It also caps the idle frame rate — from 50fps at lively down to 8fps at still — since slow motion needs no more. Hovering always restores 60.

Callouts are gated by an IntersectionObserver, so offscreen ones stop entirely. At zero the loop stops altogether. Reduced motion freezes the blobs and parks the droplet on the icon.
